Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Overview

Sweatt Shop ("we," "us," "our") operates a strength and conjugate training facility in Blue Ash, Ohio. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you use our booking form, athlete intake application, or contact form:

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address, and (where provided) age.
  • Training information: lift numbers, training experience, injury history, and performance goals, when submitted through the intake application.
  • Message content: anything you write in the contact or goal fields.

We do not use cookies for advertising or run third-party ad tracking on this site.

How We Use Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Schedule and confirm training sessions and evaluations.
  • Review athlete intake applications and build appropriate programming.
  • Respond to questions submitted through the contact form.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except where necessary to operate the gym (for example, a scheduling or email service provider acting on our behalf) or where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain submitted information only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or maintain your membership and training records, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13. Athlete intake for minors requires a parent or guardian to submit the application on the minor's behalf.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through our contact form.
