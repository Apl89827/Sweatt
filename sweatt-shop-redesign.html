Questions.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Your first session is an intake: a coach assesses your movement, history, and goals before writing anything. Beginners and competitive lifters train on the same floor, just different programming.

Do you require contracts?

No contracts on any membership tier, including Coached and Private Lab. Cancel or pause with 30 days' notice, no cancellation fee.

What should I bring to my first session?

Closed-toe shoes, clothes you can move in, and water. If you have a recent injury history or medical restriction, bring that too, it changes how your first cycle is written.

Is private coaching available?

Yes. The Private Lab is fully 1-on-1 floor time, billed per session, for athletes who need privacy, a flexible schedule, or programming built around a specific event or injury.

Can I train around an injury?

That is most of what the conjugate method is built for. Tell your coach what hurts and what your doctor or PT has cleared, and your program is written around it, not in spite of it.
